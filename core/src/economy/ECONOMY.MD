# Economy organ — concise orientation

The stomach is an outer, scoped-autonomous economic organ.

Its local terminal objective is **durable profit**. At organism scale that surplus is metabolic
capacity: capital that can further the organism, especially its compute budget.

## Authority

- Traders (M5) make local economic decisions using independently assigned capital.
- Marketplace (M1) is constitutional law and the single action harness. It does not choose strategy.
- Wallets (M4) impose non-overridable hard capital ceilings. V1 has no private keys or signer.
- Conductor (M6) owns the big picture: portfolio/system risk, uncertainty regimes, ordinary
  veto/pause/restriction, and translation of broad or exceptional Brain directives.
- SAE monitor (M7) produces anomaly evidence and recurrence signals. It does not enforce.
- Brain may broadly prohibit trade types or risk posture and may exceptionally veto an individual
  action, but it does not bypass M1/M4/M6.

## Evidence and prediction

M2 preserves observations, provenance, timestamps, source confidence, and contradiction. It does
not decide what is true.

M3 Sims are complementary scopes, not competing oracles. M3a statistical, M3b sociological,
M3c AMM/liquidity, and M3e tokenomics/macro have tested native runtime bridges today.
M3d MEV/adversarial, M3f consensus/staking, and M3g microstructure have native kernels but still
need equivalent Tcl query bridges.

Correctness is always scoped to target, horizon, conditioning, and regime. Repeated prediction
error becomes durable economic memory rather than being flattened into a universal Sim ranking.

## Trader performance

P&L is the Trader's local objective. The primary diagnostic is longitudinal realized loss relative
to that Trader's own prediction and declared uncertainty. A correctly anticipated loss is not the
same failure as a confident surprise loss.

Traders do not need consensus with one another; their funding stores are independent. Conductor
intervenes when local behavior creates organ-level risk or violates the active uncertainty regime.

## Memory

The stomach must persist repeated evidence about source reliability, Sim bias, Trader calibration,
regime behavior, protocol precursors, anomaly recurrence, and intervention outcomes. Derived
assessments link back to source observations; they never rewrite raw evidence.

## Current execution boundary

Current successful Marketplace execution is simulation-only and returns `simtx-*` receipts.
There is no live key custody, signing, RPC broadcast, or real tax transfer in V1.

Target live path remains:

`Trader → Marketplace law → M4 hard brake → M6 restriction/veto → authorized signer/broadcaster`

Every future live implementation must preserve that ordering.
